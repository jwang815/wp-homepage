The Destination

Three Countries, One Powerful Story

This journey crosses one of the most historically significant regions in the Atlantic world. In Benin, you'll explore Ganvie, the famed stilt village on Lake Nokoue, and walk through Ouidah, a city where Voodoo tradition remains deeply rooted in daily life. From sacred forests and python temples to slave-route memorials, every stop carries layers of memory and meaning.

In Togo, the itinerary moves through Lomé and Togoville, where the energy of modern West African city life meets spiritual heritage and colonial-era history. You'll experience artisanal districts, museums, the fetish market, and bustling harbors where trade and tradition converge.

The final chapter unfolds in Ghana, from Accra's Black Star Square and Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park to the coastal forts of Cape Coast and Elmina. Together, these landmarks offer an unforgettable perspective on resilience, identity, and cultural continuity across the Gulf of Guinea.

Why West Africa

Day by Day

The Expedition

Eight days through Benin, Togo, and Ghana, crafted for depth, context, and comfort.

Day 1
Arrive in Cotonou, Benin
Saturday, May 2, 2026

Arrive in Cotonou where our team will meet you at the airport. Settle in, transfer to your hotel, and receive a private briefing for the week ahead. Depending on arrival time, enjoy a relaxed coastal orientation before a welcome dinner.

Day 2
Ganvie & Abomey
Sunday, May 3, 2026

Depart Ouidah in the morning for Ganvie, the famed stilt village known as the "Venice of Africa," where roughly 35,000 residents live above the water. Continue to Abomey to visit the Royal Palace Museum, the underground village area of Bohicon, and the Chameleon Temple before returning to Ouidah for the night.

Day 3
Ouidah, Benin
Monday, May 4, 2026

Explore Ouidah, one of the spiritual homes of Voodoo and a key site in transatlantic history. Visit major slave-route monuments, the Sacred Forest used for Voodoo festivals, the Cathedral, the Temple of the Sacred Pythons, and the Gate of No Return.

Day 4
Lome & Togoville, Togo
Tuesday, May 5, 2026

Leave early for Lome and complete border formalities with assistance from the team. Visits include a former slave house in Agbodrafo, Independence Square, the Artisans Village, the National Museum, the fetish market, fishing harbor, Art Street, and the Grand Marche before overnighting in Togo.

Day 5
Drive to Accra, Ghana
Wednesday, May 6, 2026

After early border procedures, depart for Accra (approximately 4 hours 50 minutes driving time). Arrive in Ghana and check into your hotel for rest and reset. If timing allows, begin a gentle introduction to the city in the afternoon.

Day 6
Accra City Tour to Cape Coast
Thursday, May 7, 2026

Begin with a short Accra city tour including Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park, Independence (Black Star) Square, the National Museum, Osu Castle, and Jamestown. After lunch, continue westward toward Cape Coast for the next stage of the journey.

Day 7
Cape Coast & Elmina, Ghana
Friday, May 8, 2026

Depart early to explore two of Ghana's most significant coastal forts: Cape Coast Castle and Elmina Castle. Receive guided historical context throughout the day, then return to Accra in the evening.

Day 8
Return to Accra & Departure
Saturday, May 9, 2026

Enjoy a final morning in Accra before transfers to the airport for onward departure. End of services.
