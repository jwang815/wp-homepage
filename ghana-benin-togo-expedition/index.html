The Destination

Three Nations,
One Unforgettable Journey

West Africa's Gulf of Guinea coast holds some of the most profound and powerful stories on Earth. From the ancient Kingdom of Dahomey to the slave forts of Ghana's Gold Coast, this is a land where history lives in every temple, castle, and sacred grove — and where vibrant culture pulses through markets, music, and ceremony.

Benin is the birthplace of Vodun (Voodoo), a living spiritual tradition practised by millions. In Ouidah, the sacred pythons still coil in their temple, and the haunting Gate of No Return marks the final footsteps of millions sold into slavery. Offshore, the extraordinary stilt village of Ganvie — the "Venice of Africa" — rises from the waters of Lake Nokoue, home to 35,000 people who've lived on the lake for centuries.

In Togo, the fetish markets of Lomé reveal the heart of traditional West African medicine. And in Ghana, the UNESCO-listed Cape Coast and Elmina Castles stand as the most significant slave trade landmarks in the world — places of devastating history, resilience, and remembrance. From Accra's vibrant Jamestown to the royal palaces of Abomey, this journey moves through centuries of history in just eight days.

"To stand at the Gate of No Return is to understand that history is not the past — it is the ground beneath your feet."

Why This Journey

A Voyage Through Living History

Birthplace of Voodoo

Benin is where the Vodun religion originated. Visit the Temple of Sacred Pythons, the Sacred Forest, and experience a spiritual tradition practised by millions across West Africa and the diaspora.

Slave Trade Heritage

Walk through the Gate of No Return in Ouidah, explore Cape Coast Castle and Elmina Castle — UNESCO World Heritage Sites that bore witness to centuries of the transatlantic slave trade.

The Venice of Africa

Ganvie is the largest stilt village in Africa — 35,000 people living entirely on water in Lake Nokoue. Glide through floating markets and homes built on stilts, unchanged for centuries.

Royal Kingdom of Dahomey

Visit the Royal Palace Museum of Abomey, once the seat of the powerful Kingdom of Dahomey. Explore the underground village of Bohicon and the mystical Chameleon Temple.

Fetish Markets & Art

Lomé's fetish market is the largest in the world — the beating heart of traditional medicine. Browse artisan villages, Art Street, and the Grand Marché for handcrafted treasures.

Three Countries, One Journey

Cross borders from Benin to Togo to Ghana in a single expedition. Experience three distinct cultures, cuisines, and histories woven together along the Gulf of Guinea.

Day by Day

The Expedition

Eight days across three nations, from the birthplace of Voodoo to the castles of the Gold Coast.

Day 1
Arrive in Cotonou, Benin
Saturday, May 2, 2026

Arrive in Cotonou, the economic capital of Benin. Transfer to your hotel and meet the group for a welcome dinner featuring Beninese cuisine. Get briefed on the extraordinary week ahead as the sun sets over the Gulf of Guinea.

Day 2
Ganvie & Abomey
Sunday, May 3, 2026

Depart Ouidah in the morning for Ganvie, the extraordinary stilt village on Lake Nokoue — the "Venice of Africa," home to 35,000 people living entirely on water. Glide through the floating community by boat, then continue to Abomey to visit the Royal Palace Museum, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and former seat of the powerful Kingdom of Dahomey. Explore the underground village of Bohicon and the mystical Chameleon Temple before returning to Ouidah for the night.

Day 3
Ouidah — Birthplace of Voodoo
Monday, May 4, 2026

A profound day in Ouidah, the spiritual capital of Vodun (Voodoo) and one of West Africa's most significant slave trade sites. Visit the major slave route posts, the Sacred Forest used for Voodoo festivals, the Cathedral, and the Temple of the Sacred Pythons. Walk the slave trail to the haunting Gate of No Return — the final point of departure for an estimated 12 million enslaved Africans between the 16th and 19th centuries.

Day 4
Lomé, Togo — Togoville & Markets
Tuesday, May 5, 2026

Depart early for Lomé, the capital of Togo — a 90-minute drive with assisted border crossing at immigration. Visit a slave house in Agbodrafo, then explore Independence Square, the Artisans Village, and the National Museum. Discover the famous fetish market — the largest in the world and the beating heart of traditional West African medicine. End the day exploring the fishing harbour, Art Street, and the vibrant Grand Marché.

Day 5
Drive to Accra, Ghana
Wednesday, May 6, 2026

An early start for the drive to Ghana. The hotel is just 10 minutes from the Togo-Ghana border — aim to reach immigration by 7 AM for a smooth crossing. The drive from the border to Accra takes approximately five hours. Arrive in Accra, Ghana's vibrant capital, and check into your hotel for an afternoon of rest and relaxation before the days ahead.

Day 6
Accra City Tour & Drive to Cape Coast
Thursday, May 7, 2026

A morning city tour of Accra's most significant landmarks: the Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park, Independence Square and the Black Star Arch, the National Museum, Osu Castle (formerly Christiansborg Castle, the previous seat of the Ghanaian Presidency), and the colourful fishing district of Jamestown. After lunch, continue the drive south to Cape Coast.

Day 7
Cape Coast & Elmina Castles
Friday, May 8, 2026

An early departure for a day of powerful history on Ghana's Gold Coast. Visit Cape Coast Castle — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the largest of the European slave trading forts in Ghana, where captives were held in dungeons before being shipped across the Atlantic. Then explore Elmina Castle, the oldest European building in sub-Saharan Africa, built by the Portuguese in 1482. Return to Accra in the evening.

Day 8
Accra — Departure
Saturday, May 9, 2026

A leisurely final morning in Accra. Enjoy breakfast and any last-minute shopping or exploration before your transfer to Kotoka International Airport for your departure flight. Carry home not just souvenirs, but a profound connection to the history, culture, and spirit of West Africa.
